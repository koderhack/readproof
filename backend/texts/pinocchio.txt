The Adventures of Pinocchio

Gepetto was a poor woodcarver who wished for a son. One day he made a little wooden puppet and named him Pinocchio. The puppet was lively and always wanted to run away.

When the puppet was first alive, he was proud and cheeky. He laughed at the old man, then longed to go to the fair and have fun. But the path was full of tricks. A fox and a cat tried to trick him into bad choices.

Pinocchio soon learned that lying and foolishness caused trouble. His nose grew longer when he told a lie, and he had to work hard to make things right. In the end, he understood that being good and honest is the best way to become a real boy.
