The Three Little Pigs

Once upon a time, three little pigs left home to build their own houses. The first pig was lazy and built a house of straw. The second pig built a house of sticks. The third pig was wise and strong: he built a house of brick.

One day, a wolf came to the first house and blew it down. Then he went to the second house and blew that down too. But the brick house stood firm. The wolf climbed onto the roof and tried to come down the chimney, but the pigs were ready.

The wolf ran away in fright. The three pigs learned that careful work and good planning protect you better than quick and weak choices.
