The Tale of Peter Rabbit

Peter was a little rabbit with a very naughty nose. He lived in a sand-bank under the roots of a very big fir-tree with his mother and his sisters, Flopsy, Mopsy, and Cotton-tail.

One morning, Mrs. Rabbit said: "Now, my dears, you must not go into Mr. McGregor's garden. It is full of vegetables and very dangerous."

Peter was very curious. He crept under the gate and went into the garden. He nibbled a little bit of lettuce, a carrot, and some radishes. He was so busy eating that he forgot the warning.

Soon Peter met Mr. McGregor, who was very cross. Peter hurried away. He ran through the rows of beans, under the gooseberry bushes, and over a flower bed. He lost his shoes and his jacket. He was frightened and ran as fast as he could.

At last he got home. He was very tired, and his mother was upset. Peter learned that disobedience brings trouble, but he also learned that running home quickly can save you.
