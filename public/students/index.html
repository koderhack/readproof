How it works Matura preparation

Your flow

Choose a book. Read. Prove it.

  1. Choose a book

    Pick from the catalog, a reading challenge, or your teacher’s assignment.

  2. Read

    Read the real, physical book — chapter by chapter, at your pace.

  3. Take the challenge

    Answer 5 comprehension questions per chapter: characters, motives, cause and effect.

  4. Get your result

    See your score instantly, with explanations for anything you missed.

  5. Earn a ReadProof Certificate

    A certificate of demonstrated understanding with a verifiable proof ID.

Exam prep

Matura preparation & literature practice.

Use ReadProof for matura preparation and literature practice: work through set books, test your understanding, and collect ReadProof Certificates as evidence of your progress. ReadProof certificates document demonstrated understanding — they are not official exam certificates.

Certificates

Your proof, in one link.

Every passed challenge issues a certificate: book, your name, score, and a unique Certificate ID. Share the link — teachers and schools can verify it instantly.
