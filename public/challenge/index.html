How rewards work

  1. Publisher selects a book

    …and creates a Reading Challenge.

    Fixed amount, locked by predefined rules.

  3. Readers participate

    Read the book, complete chapter challenges.

  4. ReadProof verifies understanding

    Only verified proofs qualify.

  5. Rewards are distributed

    Automatically, by the published rules.
