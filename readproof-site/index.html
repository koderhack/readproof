Reading is easy to claim.
Understanding is harder to prove.

Each chapter ends with 5 short questions about meaning — characters, motives, cause and effect. Never trivia. Here's the idea:

Example · Alice in Wonderland, Ch. 1

Why does Alice follow the White Rabbit?

  • ACuriosity — a talking rabbit in a waistcoat is too strange to ignore
  • BShe wants to steal his pocket watch
  • CHer sister tells her to go after it
  • DShe is late for tea and hopes he knows the way
Correct — and the app explains why, so a miss becomes a lesson, not just a score.

Comprehension, not memory

Questions check whether you understood the story — not whether you memorized details.

5 questions per chapter

Mixed types: choices, who-said-it, ordering, open answers judged for meaning.
