Inside a challenge

Reading is easy to claim.
Understanding is harder to prove.

Each chapter ends with 5 short questions about meaning — characters, motives, cause and effect. Never trivia like “what color was the watch”. Here’s the idea:

Example · Alice in Wonderland, Ch. 1

Why does Alice follow the White Rabbit?

  • ACuriosity — a talking rabbit in a waistcoat is too strange to ignore
  • BShe wants to steal his pocket watch
  • CHer sister tells her to go after it
  • DShe is late for tea and hopes he knows the way
Correct — and the app explains why, so a miss becomes a lesson, not just a score.

Comprehension, not memory

Questions check whether you understood the story — not whether you memorized details.

5 questions per chapter

Mixed types: choices, who-said-it, ordering, open answers judged for meaning.

Pass it, prove it

Your score becomes a verifiable certificate — one link, checkable by anyone.

Certificates

A certificate of demonstrated understanding.

Finish a challenge and receive a ReadProof Certificate with your score and a verifiable proof ID. Anyone — a teacher, a school, an employer — can check it with one link.

Under the hood

Proof you can trust, infrastructure you never notice.

Every verified result is recorded as a tamper-evident proof. You read, take challenges and earn certificates — verification just works in the background.

  • ✓ Challenge completed & understanding verified
  • ✓ Proof recorded — checkable by ID in Proof Details
  • Verified on-chain Network: Solana Devnet · Proof ID · Transaction

Demo environment: test network, no real funds. Book texts and answers are never stored on-chain — only the proof hash.
