Your flow

Five steps to a class that reads.

  1. Create a class

    Add your students once — e.g. Class 2A, 28 students.

  2. Assign a book

    Choose a title and set a deadline for the challenge.

  3. Students complete challenges

    They read the real book and prove understanding chapter by chapter.

  4. See results

    Completion rate, average score, per-student results and certificates.

  5. Identify who needs help

    Focus your lessons where understanding is weakest.
