Your flow

From manuscript to measurable readers.

  1. Select a book

    Your title or a public-domain classic — with ISBN and cover.

  2. Create a Reading Challenge

    Name, description, dates, participant limit, eligibility and reward rules.

  3. Fund the reward pool

    A fixed amount per challenge. Details in the panel — settled on Solana Devnet while in demo.

  4. Readers participate

    They read the real book and complete chapter challenges.

  5. ReadProof verifies understanding

    Meaning-checked answers, anti-cheat on device. Only verified proofs qualify.

  6. Rewards are distributed

    Automatically, according to the rules you published.
